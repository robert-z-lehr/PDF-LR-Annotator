Controls

1–7 switch the active category. Enter jumps to the next highlight and Shift+Enter to the previous one, with wraparound. Hover over a highlight for its action hint. Single-click opens its comment; double-click removes it. Esc cancels a two-click start point.

Category reference

Pastel red · Background / Theory
Prior knowledge, mechanisms, established relationships, conceptual framing

Pastel orange · Research Question / Hypothesis
The study aim, research gap, explicit questions

Pastel yellow · Methodology
Data collection, instruments, study design, variables, statistical methods

Pastel green · Results / Data
Numerical findings and direct empirical observations

Pastel blue · Analysis / Interpretation
What the authors think the findings mean

Pastel indigo · Conclusion / Implications
Main takeaway, planning/design relevance, recommendations

Pastel violet · Limitations / Future Work
Weaknesses, uncertainty, methodological constraints, proposed next research
